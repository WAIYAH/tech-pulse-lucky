## 💡 Success Tips

1. **Read, don't memorize.** These are reference documents, not textbooks.

2. **Update metrics weekly.** Data informs better decisions than opinions.

3. **Monthly reviews matter.** 2 hours of reflection prevents months of wasted effort.

4. **Share progress, not plans.** Talk about what you're doing, not what you might do.

5. **Stay focused.** Say no to 99 good ideas. Build 1 great idea really well.

6. **Celebrate wins.** Hit $2k revenue? Celebrate. 1k email subscribers? Celebrate.

7. **Be honest about setbacks.** If something isn't working, change it. Fast.

8. **Trust the process.** 90 days of consistent execution beats 1 year of unfocused dabbling.
