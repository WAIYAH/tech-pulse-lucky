## What NOT to Build

**❌ User Accounts** – No login required. Free content should be frictionless.  
**❌ Mobile App** – Website works on mobile. Apps add maintenance burden.  
**❌ Discussion Forums** – WhatsApp handles community better. Forums are dead.  
**❌ Paywalls on Articles** – Free = trust. Articles build audience for paid offers.  
**❌ Live Chat** – Email + WhatsApp sufficient. Live chat doesn't scale.  
**❌ Video Infrastructure** – YouTube hosts. Don't reinvent.  
**❌ Marketplace** – Stay founder-led. Multi-instructor platforms have quality issues.  
**❌ Ads** – Wrong audience, kills UX. Paid courses are cleaner revenue.

## Key Principles

1. **Trust is your moat.** Everything else is secondary. Protect it fiercely.

2. **Simplicity scales.** Every feature you don't build is a feature you don't maintain.

3. **Content is king.** Articles are your strongest asset. Repurpose, don't create more.

4. **Community is real.** WhatsApp engagement > forum activity. Stay authentic.

5. **Focus drives growth.** Say no to 99 good ideas to say yes to 1 great idea.

6. **Data beats opinions.** Track metrics. Be honest about what works.

7. **Revenue enables mission.** Make money to sustain the platform. Money isn't the goal, but it's required.

8. **Africa-first, not Africa-only.** Build for Africans (mobile, M-Pesa, data costs). This unlocks global appeal.

## Decision Framework

When you're unsure whether to build something, ask:

1. **Does it help users learn?** (Yes → continue)
2. **Can I build it in <1 week?** (Yes → continue)
3. **Does it require accounts/apps?** (No → continue)
4. **Does it increase revenue?** (Yes → build)
5. **Can I delete it later if it fails?** (Yes → build)

**If any answer is wrong → Don't build it.**
